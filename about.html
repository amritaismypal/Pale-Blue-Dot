About Pale Blue Dot

Pale Blue Dot is an interdisciplinary journal that centers student scholars and intellectuals outside the United States. We aim to create a space where international students can publish their work fairly and responsibly.

Mission

Our mission is to platform original, politically conscious research that challenges dominant narratives and expands global knowledge production. We publish work in the author's original language and in English.

First Edition — Chile

Our inaugural issue focuses on Chile, featuring student research, translations, and conversations that center Chilean perspectives.
